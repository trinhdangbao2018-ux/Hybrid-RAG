# Third-party content

`documents/d2l/` contains chapters of *Dive into Deep Learning* by Aston Zhang,
Zachary C. Lipton, Mu Li and Alexander J. Smola (https://d2l.ai), taken from
https://github.com/d2l-ai/d2l-en at commit `23d7a5a` (2024-03-16).

Chapters included: Appendix: Mathematics for Deep Learning, Preliminaries,
Linear Regression, Optimization Algorithms.

Modification: in every multi-framework block only the PyTorch variant was kept;
the MXNet, TensorFlow and JAX variants were removed.
